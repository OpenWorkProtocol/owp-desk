# Denver → Salt Lake City

kind: lane
id: DEN-SLC
miles: 525
transit: 1 day
equipment: dry van, reefer
backhaul: SLC-DEN runs light; price the round trip, not the leg

The spine of the western book. I-80 over Wyoming when the weather is honest,
I-70 over Vail when it is not — and neither is free in February.

## Standing facts
- Chains are required over Vail Pass from November to April; a driver without them turns around at Silverthorne.
- Wyoming closes I-80 for wind at least twice a winter. A load parked for a closure is parked, not late.
